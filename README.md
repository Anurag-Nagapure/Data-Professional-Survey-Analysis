
# Data Professional Survey Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes survey responses from data professionals to understand salary trends, job roles, programming language preferences, geographic distribution, and career satisfaction.

Using Microsoft Power BI, I built an interactive dashboard to explore the data professional landscape and identify patterns related to compensation, preferred programming languages, work-life balance, salary satisfaction, and the perceived difficulty of entering the data industry.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The data industry offers various career paths, each with different salary levels, technical skills, and professional experiences. Understanding these differences can help aspiring data professionals make informed career decisions and identify relevant skills to learn.

The objective of this project is to analyze survey data and answer key questions about job roles, salaries, programming language preferences, and career satisfaction.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze average salary across different data-related job titles.
- Identify the most popular programming languages among survey respondents.
- Explore the geographic distribution of data professionals.
- Examine average age and survey respondent count.
- Evaluate salary satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Understand how respondents perceive the difficulty of entering the data industry.
- Present key findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.

## 🔍 Key Findings

Based on the Power BI dashboard:

- **Survey Participants:** The dashboard reports 630 survey takers.
- **Average Age:** The average age of respondents is 29.87 years.
- **Salary by Job Title:** Data Scientist appears as the highest salary category in the displayed comparison.
- **Programming Language:** Python has the highest respondent count among the programming language categories shown.
- **Work-Life Balance:** The average satisfaction rating is 5.74 out of 10.
- **Salary Satisfaction:** The average satisfaction rating is 4.27 out of 10.
- **Career Entry Difficulty:** The largest displayed group, 42.7% of respondents, considered entering the data field neither easy nor difficult.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Career Planning:** Aspiring data professionals can use the job-role comparison as a starting point when researching career options.
2. **Skill Development:** Python may be worth prioritizing when building a learning plan, alongside the skills required for a specific target role.
3. **Salary Satisfaction:** Further analysis by job title, country, and experience level could help explain differences in salary satisfaction.
4. **Work-Life Balance:** Organizations could examine satisfaction across roles and industries to identify areas for improvement.
5. **Career Readiness:** Training providers could investigate barriers to entering the data industry and offer practical projects, mentoring, and interview preparation.
